# Two Lines to Your Past Self

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty **Two Lines to Your Past Self** ($2 reward, 1st place takes all). The program writes a two-line warning to the writer's past self: line one warns about one specific moment, and line two says why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

> Don't push the deploy on Friday at 4:55 p.m. just because the tests are green and the office is already empty.
> The rollback eats your whole weekend, and Monday's stand-up is where you first hear the words 'change freeze' -- from your own manager.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the two-line entry to `/output/entry.txt` (1–280 characters, exactly 2 lines, validated before writing).
